## Why This Source Matters

- This is the clean legal list that declares which diseases are officially notifiable in Namibia and for which animals.
- It is strong enough for:
  - gold MCQ labeling when the claim is "this disease is legally notifiable"
  - susceptible-species mapping
  - pairing with reporting-duty sources
- Main limitation:
  - it does not tell the farmer what to do after suspicion
  - it does not give clinical signs, first aid, or treatment guidance
  - pair it with `SRC-AHA-REG-2018` for reporting/isolation action
