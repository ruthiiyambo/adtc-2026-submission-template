A simple animal help chat for farmers. Ask about any farm animal. Get clear steps you can try at home first — with a plain warning only when you truly need a vet.

Field use

Built for long travel distances, dry-season stress, and low-connectivity farm work.

Cattle

Bloat, weakness, fever, no appetite

Goats

Diarrhea, pale eyelids, coughing, limpness

Sheep

Swelling, breathing trouble, poor feeding

Calves

Scours, weakness, dehydration, cold stress

These are just common examples. You can ask about any farm animal — cattle, goats, sheep, calves, donkeys, pigs, chickens, and more.

Best way to ask

  • Say the animal
  • Say what you see
  • Say when it started
  • Say if more than one animal is sick
