# Farmer Notes

This folder is for phrasing, concerns, and context gathered from farmer conversations.

## Use it for

- real wording that should shape prompts and MCQs
- recurring pain points and misconceptions worth addressing
- season, species, and management context that makes examples feel local

## Do not treat it as

- stand-alone veterinary truth
- a substitute for a cited extension or veterinary source

## Good capture habits

- note the date, region, species, and summary of the concern
- record whether consent was given for saving notes
- remove names, phone numbers, and other identifying details when they are not essential
- carry useful phrasing into both SFT and MCQ writing after vetting the underlying fact
